dtcg-contrast

Gate WCAG 2.2 and APCA contrast on your design tokens.

A zero-dependency CLI and GitHub Action that checks W3C DTCG 2025.10 design tokens for WCAG 2.2 and APCA contrast, and auto-fixes failing colors in OKLCH.

  • Quick start→
  • $npm i dtcg-contrast

Highlights

  • Checks every foreground and background pair in WCAG 2.2 and APCA, in light and dark.

  • Auto-fixes failing colors in OKLCH while keeping the hue.

  • Runs as a CLI or a GitHub Action with no dependencies.
